<div class="cfp-section">
  <h2>SQAI 2026 — Invited Speaker Series</h2>
  <p>SQAI 2026 featured an <strong>invited speaker series</strong> rather than an open call for papers. The program consisted of curated talks from leading experts in quantum AI, selected by the organizing committee.</p>
</div>

<div class="cfp-section">
  <h2>SQAI 2027 has an open Call for Papers</h2>
  <p>SQAI 2027 introduces <strong>SQAI-A</strong>, our first peer-reviewed academic conference track. See the <a href="https://sqai.org/2027/cfp/">SQAI 2027 Call for Papers</a> for submission topics, dates, and details.</p>
</div>

<div class="cfp-section">
  <h2>Past Proceedings</h2>
  <p>SQAI 2021 and SQAI 2023 were held as workshop and seminar series without formal proceedings. SQAI 2027 will be the first edition to produce citable proceedings.</p>
  <p>For recordings and summaries from past events, see the <a href="https://sqai.org/past/">Past Events</a> page.</p>
</div>
