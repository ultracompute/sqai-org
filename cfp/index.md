## SQAI 2026 — No Call for Papers

SQAI 2026 features an **invited speaker series** rather than an open call for papers. The program consists of 3–5 curated talks from leading experts in quantum AI, selected by the organizing committee.

If you are interested in being considered as a speaker for a future edition, please contact us at [info@sqai.org](mailto:info@sqai.org).

---

## SQAI 2027 — Call for Papers (Forthcoming)

We plan to issue a full **Call for Papers for SQAI 2027**, provisionally scheduled for **July 20, 2027**.

Topics of interest will include (but are not limited to):

- Quantum machine learning algorithms and theory
- Quantum neural networks and optimization
- Quantum natural language processing
- Hybrid classical–quantum AI systems
- Quantum hardware for AI workloads
- Fault-tolerant quantum computation and AI
- Quantum-inspired classical AI methods
- Benchmarking and evaluation of quantum AI systems
- Societal, ethical, and policy dimensions of quantum AI

**CFP details will be announced on this page and via our mailing list.**

To be notified when the CFP opens, follow us on social media or email [info@sqai.org](mailto:info@sqai.org) with "SQAI 2027 CFP" in the subject line.
