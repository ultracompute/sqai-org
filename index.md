## About SQAI 2026

The **International Summit on Quantum Artificial Intelligence (SQAI)** is the world's premier gathering dedicated to the intersection of quantum computing and artificial intelligence. Founded in 2021 by the [Ultracompute Institute](https://sqi.tw), SQAI brings together leading researchers, technologists, and visionaries to explore how quantum computation can unlock the next frontier of intelligence.

SQAI 2026 is the **3rd edition** of this landmark summit.

## Hosted by the Ultracompute Institute

The [Ultracompute Institute](https://sqi.tw) (UCI) is a Washington, D.C. nonprofit organization dedicated to advancing quantum software research and building the world's first ultracomputer for Artificial Ultraintelligence (AUI). UCI founded SQAI in 2021 as the world's first academic conference dedicated to quantum AI.
