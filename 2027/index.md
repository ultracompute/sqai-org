## About SQAI 2027

The **International Summit on Quantum Artificial Intelligence (SQAI)** 2027 is the **4th edition** of the summit founded in 2021 — and its biggest yet. For the first time, SQAI expands into three connected tracks under one banner:

## Why Three Tracks?

SQAI 2027 is structured around three audiences that quantum AI needs to bring together:

- **SQAI-A: Research & Technology** — researchers, papers, peer review, technical talks.
- **SQAI-B: Industry & Innovation Expo** — where quantum AI meets the market: companies, startups, demos, partnerships.
- **SQAI-C: Capital & Policy Summit** — where quantum AI meets capital, government, and national strategy, with linked sessions in Washington, DC and Taipei.

---

## Looking Further Ahead

SQAI was founded in 2021 as the world's first academic conference dedicated to quantum AI. Our aim is to keep growing this summit into a lasting international institution — one we intend to keep hosting well into the 2030s and beyond.
