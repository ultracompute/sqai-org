<div class="cfp-section">
  <h2>SQAI-A 2027 — Call for Papers</h2>
  <p>SQAI 2027 marks a new chapter for SQAI: <strong>SQAI-A</strong>, our first track with a formal, peer-reviewed Call for Papers, held in person at <strong>National Taiwan University on July 20, 2027</strong>.</p>
  <p>Submissions are reviewed by a Technical Program Committee. Accepted papers will be presented at SQAI-A and published in SQAI's proceedings.</p>
</div>

<div class="cfp-section">
  <h3>Topics of Interest</h3>
  <p>SQAI-A welcomes original research contributions including (but not limited to):</p>
  <ul class="topic-list">
    <li>Quantum machine learning algorithms and theory</li>
    <li>Quantum neural networks and optimization</li>
    <li>Quantum natural language processing</li>
    <li>Hybrid classical–quantum AI systems</li>
    <li>Quantum hardware for AI workloads</li>
    <li>Fault-tolerant quantum computation and AI</li>
    <li>Quantum-inspired classical AI methods</li>
    <li>Benchmarking and evaluation of quantum AI systems</li>
    <li>Societal, ethical, and policy dimensions of quantum AI</li>
  </ul>
</div>

<div class="cfp-section">
  <h3>Review Process &amp; Publication</h3>
  <p>Papers are reviewed by our Technical Program Committee. Registration fees, submission deadlines, and publication details are being finalized and will be posted here as they're confirmed.</p>
  <p>To be notified when submissions open, email <a href="mailto:info@sqai.org">info@sqai.org</a> with <strong>SQAI-A 2027 CFP</strong> in the subject line.</p>
</div>

<div class="cfp-section">
  <h2>Past Proceedings</h2>
  <p>SQAI 2021, SQAI 2023, and SQAI 2026 were held as invited talks, workshops, and seminar series without formal proceedings. SQAI-A 2027 will be the first edition to produce citable proceedings.</p>
  <p>For recordings and summaries from past events, see the <a href="https://sqai.org/past/">Past Events</a> page.</p>
</div>
